# MK Location — Prompts de génération

Outil intégré `image_gen` ; six appels, une image par sujet. Aucun CLI/API externe.

## Mini-pelle — image maîtresse

Référence : flyer utilisateur (catégorie/silhouette uniquement).

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural. Create ONE original high-end editorial equipment photograph for French rental company MK Location's existing MK / DEPLOYED website. Not a collage, not a flyer, no layout or text.
Reference role: the attached flyer is only evidence of the equipment category and silhouette: yellow compact mini excavator with black open canopy, rubber tracks, front dozer blade and articulated boom. Reinterpret the equipment in an entirely new photographic setting; do not reuse or crop flyer pixels, branding, black-gold stage or composition.
Scene: quiet architect-designed industrial bay, raw pale warm-gray concrete walls and floor, subtle horizontal construction joints, a deep muted petroleum-green shadow at far right. Restrained real material texture, no signage, no props, no other equipment.
Subject: one realistic compact yellow and black mini excavator with open rollover canopy, black operator seat, two rubber tracks, hydraulic cylinders and believable hoses, steel bucket grounded gently to camera-left, blade, no operator. Mechanically coherent, clean well-maintained working equipment, tiny honest wear on bucket and tracks. No make/model claims, no fake markings.
Composition: landscape 3:2, ideally 1536x1024. Full machine within frame with 12 percent clear breathing room on every side, three-quarter view showing left side, bucket and front blade, vehicle fills about 72 percent of image width. Camera at machine waist height, 50mm editorial photography, controlled perspective. Clear dark contact shadow, matte floor with only faint satin reflection.
Lighting: large soft daylight source upper-left, graceful falloff toward right, gentle muted petrol fill in shadows, strong readable silhouette, natural warm yellow machine paint. Warm mineral highlights (bone #F1EEE5), charcoal #171A18 shadows. Neutral photographic grading, realistic not cinematic haze, no dramatic spotlight, no orange filter, no oversaturation.
Modern premium industrial campaign, exquisite metal/rubber/glass detail, sharp subject, deep natural depth of field, physically plausible photography, no CGI appearance, no cutout, no logo, no watermarks, no text, no borders. This will be the lighting, lens and environment master for a coherent six-subject series.
```
